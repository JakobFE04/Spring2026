## a)
The divergence theorem states that the divergence of a vector field $v$ in a given volume $V$ should be equal to the dot product of the vector field with the normal surface vector across the surface (flux). This means that the change in a vector field inside of a volumetric area is equal to the amount of the vector field crossing the surface of the surface of the area. Can explain this as the flow of water through a cube in a river, the rate of flow inside (divergence) wil be equal to the amount of water that goes in/out of the cubes surfaces.
