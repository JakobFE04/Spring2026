1. Core & Algorithmic Signal Processing

These modules are hosted primarily within the [Graduate School of Informatics](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/international/) and focus on data conversion, filtering, and mathematical optimization: [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/about/staff/dci/), [2](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dci/about/)]

- **Intelligent Signal Processing / Signal Processing Engineering:** Focuses on the core algorithmic approaches to signal manipulation, sampling theorems, digital filters (FIR/IIR), and time-series extraction. [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/about/staff/dci/), [2](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dci/about/)]
- **Image Processing / Intelligent Media Processing:** Covers multidimensional signal processing, image extraction, spatial filtering, noise reduction, and video data rendering. [, [2](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dci/about/)]
- **Pattern Recognition & Machine Learning:** Explores downstream signal processing applications, focusing on converting raw hardware sensor data into classification vectors. [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dci/about/), [2](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dii/about/)]

2. Communication Signals & Networking

These courses bridge the gap between raw hardware electronics and information data packet manipulation: [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/eng/en/undergraduate/elect-elect-syst-eng/)]

- **Information Sensing and Wireless Telecommunications:** Evaluates how physical physical antennas and electronic transceivers modulate, transmit, and clean electromagnetic wave signals. [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/i/en/dii/about/)]
- **Digital Communication Engineering:** Concentrates on modulation schemes (e.g., QAM, OFDM), signal degradation, channel equalization, and error-correcting codes used in electronics hardware.
- **Mobile and Wireless Networks:** Focuses on signal traffic routing, protocols, and spatial multiplexing across modern wireless architectures. [[1](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-031-87781-0.pdf)]

3. Electronics Hardware & Sensor Interfacing

These modules are hosted within the [Department of Electrical and Electronic Systems Engineering](https://www.omu.ac.jp/eng/en/undergraduate/elect-elect-syst-eng/) and deal with the physical systems that generate or receive signals: [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/eng/en/undergraduate/elect-elect-syst-eng/)]

- **Sensing and Measurement Systems:** Covers the electronic front-ends of hardware, exploring how real-world physical anomalies are converted into measurable voltage and current signals. [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/eng/en/undergraduate/elect-elect-syst-eng/)]
- **Optoelectronic Engineering / Photonic Sensing:** Focuses on processing optical signals, fiber optics, laser modulation, and sensor integration for electro-optical instruments. [[1](https://www.omu.ac.jp/eng/en/undergraduate/elect-elect-syst-eng/), [2](https://www-ap.eng.osaka-u.ac.jp/graduate/en/curriculum/)]
- **System Control Engineering:** Applies signal processing feedback loops to physical systems, focusing on automated hardware control systems, stabilization filters, and state estimation.
